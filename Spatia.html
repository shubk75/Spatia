How it works

1 Standardize

Each photo is cropped to a square and projected at a matching focal length so it fills its angular sector without stretching.

2 Align

Photos are spaced evenly around 360°, with each one's edge meeting the next seamlessly.

3 Project

Every point on the sphere is sampled from a single source photo, so nothing is blended or ghosted.
